About Me

I am a first year Statistics PhD student at Trinity College Dublin.

My PhD is a part of the AIM CP project, an interdisciplinary initiative with the goal of improving outcomes for children with Cerebral Palsy. This is funded by the Trinity Research Doctorate Award (TRDA).

My research explores indicators of health and well-being in children with Cerebral Palsy, using methods such as Latent Class Analysis to identify meaningful subgroups within the population and better understand variation in outcomes.

I am under the primary supervision of Dr. Arthur White, with Dr. Jason Wyse acting as a co-supervisor.

I graduated from my bachelors in Mathematics in 2024, and was awarded a gold medal for academic achievement.
